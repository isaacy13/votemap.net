# VoteMap Vision

VoteMap means **vote + roadmap**: money becomes a tool people use to signal and fund the futures they actually want.

It is an open platform for directing real money and continuous public pressure toward concrete outcomes, ideas, and entities. It does **not** replace elections — it sits on top of them as an ongoing accountability and prioritization layer.

## What users can do

1. **Stake money on specific outcomes**  
   Anyone can create or fund a measurable goal. Each contribution carries a **personal deadline** set by the user. If the outcome is delivered, the money is released to those who made it happen. If the deadline expires without delivery, the money **automatically returns** to the user.

2. **Make direct donations** to entities, causes, nonprofits, creators, churches, or officials — under the same transparency rules.

3. **Entities post bounties** — companies, agencies, startups, and creators list problems or goals and invite the public to fund or work on them.

## Core principles

- **Radical transparency** — every transaction is public and explained (public ledger).
- **Strong identity + reputation** — passport-based verification via open-source **ZKPassport** (zero-knowledge proofs from NFC passport chips). Profiles can show age band, region, last verification, and optional name.
- **Face ID to vote** — every stake, donation, or resolution requires device biometrics. On web, users confirm with **Face ID on their phone** before the action proceeds.
- **Continuous, issue-level input** — people keep directing capital at specific results after elections.

## Broader goals

Ideaocracy (great ideas shape the future), meritocracy (implementers get rewarded), and a closer form of continuous democracy — without replacing the ballot box.
