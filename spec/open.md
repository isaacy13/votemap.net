# Open questions

Not locked. Solved (v0 only): [requirements.md](./requirements.md). Known accepted risks: [accepted-risks.md](./accepted-risks.md). Later (not solved, unfinished): [later.md](./later.md).

| | | Proposed solutions (not locked) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fees vs behavior | Pay 1.5%. Pledge, cancel, and lapse 0%. The only fee sits on the wanted action (paying). A voter can skip it by sending USDC directly, which then does not count as a votemap pay: no trail row, no follow-through credit, no payee lookup. Unsettled: whether follow-through and the trail are worth 1.5% to payers, or whether pays route around the router. **No new fee numbers.** |  |
| Catalog is not the charge | The catalog is off-chain. A shop pay names a product and a price, and the signed pay carries both. The chain cannot see the list. The buyer can be shown one price while the merchant changed the catalog. After the tap, the pay is final. | 1. The signer refuses a pay whose price does not match that product’s current catalog price. 2. The named price can differ from the catalog. The confirm screen is the check. |
| Payout wallet moves | Mapping a wallet takes a signature from that wallet. Nothing says the current payout wallet has to sign to move payout. A taken Google or Apple session can map a new wallet and set it as payout. After 24 hours, the next pay goes to it. | 1. The current payout wallet has to sign before payout moves. 2. A logged-in session can set payout to a wallet it mapped at least 24 hours ago. |
| Lost phone | Pledge and pay need the phone app and biometrics. Nothing lets a replacement phone enroll. No USDC is stranded (it stays in the wallet), but the account cannot pledge or pay through votemap. | 1. A new phone can enroll for the same account. A taken Google or Apple login can enroll one too. 2. The original phone is the only signer. |
| One post, two pots | X stores one pot per status id. Instagram allows `/p/{code}`, `/reel/{code}`, and `/{user}/p/{code}`, and those are not folded together. `threads.com` and `threads.net` are both allowed and are not folded together. The same post can be two pots, splitting its pledges. | 1. The same Instagram code is one pot key across `/p/`, `/reel/`, and `/{user}/p/`. `threads.com` is stored as `threads.net`. 2. Each of those URL shapes stays its own pot. |
| Post is edited | After pledges are in, the author can change the caption. The issue page still shows the live embed. Browse keeps the first-pledge line. New pledges hit the same pot against the new text. | 1. The issue page keeps showing the live embed after the caption changes. 2. When the live caption differs from the first pledge, the card stays the snapshot. |
| Post goes private or deleted after money is in | Onlookers can’t see what the money was for. Pledgers look stuck. | 1. Keep the first-pledge snapshot as the card forever. Live embed shows unavailable. Pledges and pays unchanged. 2. Refuse new pledges. Pays still go through. 3. Do both. |
| No stop | Hiding a row in the browse index leaves the pot payable. A post used to harass someone, or a shop listing an illegal good, can keep taking pays. | 1. Pots and merchants stay payable. A hidden browse row does not stop pays. 2. The signer refuses new pledges and pays on a pot or merchant it marks. USDC stays in voters’ wallets. |
| Illegal-only moderation | Stop only what is strictly illegal: drugs, trafficking, paying for votes, and the same class of thing. Not a general content ban. Moderation has to be completely open. People can still see a muted post. The record of who muted it and why is public. Whether that record is on-chain is not decided. Paying public officials and paying for votes are already blocked in [requirements](./requirements.md) item 27. | 1. Write the mute on-chain: who, which post, and why. The card stays readable and shows that it was muted. 2. Keep the same public record off-chain. Still not a hidden ban list. |
| Wrong network | The pay picker is a verified social on that pot’s network. The reward is for the person who did the work. That person can be verified only on one of the other three, or on none. They cannot be the payee. | 1. The payee has to be verified on the pot’s network. 2. Any verified social on the four networks can be the payee. The bind method per network stays. |
| No taxpayer | A pay moves USDC from the voter’s wallet to a person. Legal name is not collected. The trail has a handle and a wallet. With no custody, the voter is the payer, and whether votemap settles anything is weaker than under the old factory, but votemap still signs each pay and takes a fee. Research on what the law may require: [legal-reporting.md](./legal-reporting.md), written for the old factory. |  |
| Rename splits the pot | Threads and TikTok pot keys include the @. A rename makes a new URL, so the same post is a second pot. X can key the status id, so a rename there does not split the pot. | 1. Threads and TikTok keys keep the post id and drop the @, the way X can key the status id. 2. The @ stays in the key. A rename is a second pot. |
| Contract wallet | Mapping a wallet takes a signature from that address. That step has no contract-wallet check. An address that cannot produce a key signature cannot be mapped, pledge, pay, or be the payout. | 1. Only a key signature maps a wallet. A contract wallet cannot map, pledge, pay, or be the payout. 2. A signature the contract wallet itself verifies (ERC-1271) can map it, and that address can pledge, pay, and be the payout. |
| Trail social moves | The trail row stores the votemap handle from that moment. Social names are filled from the pay lookup. Nothing freezes the social @ on the row. After a rebind, an old pay can show a different Instagram, X, Threads, or TikTok. | 1. The row stores the social @ from that moment. A rebind does not change it. 2. The page uses the account’s current binds. Old pays show the new social. |
| No age check | Signup stores a birth year. The value is a year, not a day. Nothing reads it to block pledge, pay, or shop register. Coinbase accounts are 18+, and pledge, pay, and payee all need the Coinbase badge. | 1. Under 18 cannot pledge, pay, register a shop, or be a payee. 2. The stored year does not block the money path. |
| Wash pays | Two real Coinbase accounts can pay each other on a pot to look like a claim others agreed with. That moves other pledgers’ follow-through and costs 1.5% per pass. Same-account block does not see them. | 1. Accept. The trail shows the round trip. 2. A pay counts toward “another account paid a claimant” only above a share of that pot’s backed total. |
| Pledge reliability | A pledge is a promise with no escrow. Backed amount and follow-through are the only weight behind it. A new account has no follow-through yet. Unsettled: whether solvers trust backed plus follow-through enough to start work. | 1. Show follow-through as “new” until an account has ended enough pledges to count. 2. Show the raw number from the first pledge. |

v0 fee numbers stay in [requirements](./requirements.md). This is not later. No new fee schedule.
