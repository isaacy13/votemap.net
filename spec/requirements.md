Open questions: [open.md](./open.md). Known accepted risks: [accepted-risks.md](./accepted-risks.md). Later (not solved, unfinished): [later.md](./later.md).

Locked with Isaac, 2026-09-26 (credits + vest ≤365d 2026-09-28; bio bind 2026-10-02; shop place + public page 2026-10-02; named money trail 2026-10-03; shop catalog 2026-10-03; redeem is final 2026-10-03; legal name not collected, website verification, and the 60-day lag 2026-10-03; outcome payee and OFAC via Coinbase 2026-10-03; shop bounty eligibility 2026-10-09; exact bio token, bio URL host, and cloaked website string 2026-10-09; profile bio read and paced daily job 2026-10-09; secure phone confirm 2026-10-09; shop website URL https and same host 2026-10-09; already claimed and failed tag check 2026-10-09; client-then-server URL check and 24-hour same-URL limit 2026-10-09; no custody, pledge and direct pay, no PII on-chain, isolated fetcher, signature replay fix, one payee per pay, and no payouts to public officials 2026-10-19). This is the **solved v0** page. Production: [PR #102](https://github.com/isaacy13/votemap.net/pull/102). [PR #101](https://github.com/isaacy13/votemap.net/pull/101) is **stopped**. Diagrams: [flows](./flows.md). The user-flow and system-flow PNGs are not in this repo. Cheats that are locked: [cheats-and-fixes.md](./cheats-and-fixes.md).

**Vote** = pledge USDC on an **X, Threads, Instagram, or TikTok** post, then pay the person who did the work straight from your own wallet. Pledges are for an outcome. The reward goes to the person who did the work (builder, writer, shop, advocate). v0 does not file lobbying disclosures (LDA or otherwise). No lobbying-filing product. A public official, a candidate, their staff or immediate family, or a wallet or shop they control, cannot be paid (item 27).

**No custody (solved v0, 2026-10-19).** VoteMap never holds USDC. There is no factory balance, no escrow, no shop credit, and no vest. USDC stays in the voter’s wallet until that voter pays. A pay moves USDC from the voter’s wallet straight to the payee’s wallet in the voter’s own transaction. “Pledge” means a public, wallet-signed promise to pay up to an amount toward a post’s outcome before a date. It is not a deposit, not DeFi / yield / validator staking, and not a contract balance. Pay is final: VoteMap cannot refund, reverse, or freeze it, because VoteMap never had it.

**Incentives (solved v0).** Without escrow the pledge is the signal and reputation is the commitment. Item 10 has the rules. In short: pledging and cancelling are free, so signalling is cheap; a pot shows **backed** dollars, never more than pledgers actually hold; every account shows its **follow-through** (did it pay when others agreed the work was done); the only fee is on pay, so VoteMap earns only when a solver gets paid.

## Account (Google / Apple)

1. Sign in with **Google** or **Apple** (Isaac already pays Apple Developer).
2. Save OIDC **`sub`**, **email**, **name**. Key by `sub`, not email. Apple **name only on first Allow** — must save then. Email may be Hide My Email. **Legal name is not collected and is not a signup field.** v0 does not require a legal name to sign up. The OIDC `name` is the provider display name, not a legal name.
3. Then a votemap form: **gender**, **birth year**, **phone + SMS OTP**. Google/Apple do **not** provide gender/age. No handle until OTP succeeds.
4. Unique votemap **handle** (first-come) **and unique E.164**. One phone → one account, kept if they change handle. SMS vendor: reject VoIP when flagged. Handles are **FCFS**. Squat-the-name is **v0-ok** (votemap `@nike` is theirs). It does **not** confer Instagram `@nike`. **Pay picker is verified social @, not votemap @.**
5. **Wallet is optional.** No wallet = **readonly** (browse, edit own profile). Cannot post, pledge, or pay until a wallet is linked.
6. **Public page** at **`votemap.net/@username`** (votemap handle). Linktree-like. They customize which links show (labels, order, what is listed). They can **show their shop** on this page as well as links. **v0 shop is a catalog**, not only an address on the profile. Shops list products they sell. A product can be physical or not. Each listed product: **name**, **price in USDC**, **physical or not**, **short text**. Off-chain, like links. Not a token. Not shipping labels, tax, themes, inventory sync, or staff accounts. License stays **later**. Hiding a social on this page does **not** unbind it. Pay picker still uses the bio bind. Bio text stays **`votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle`**. This page is the public URL. Paying a shop: item 9.

    **Verified marks (v0):** only TikTok, X, Threads, and Instagram, via the bio rule (`votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle` in that account bio, 60 days, daily job on due binds only), and a website only if that site itself shows `votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle` (same two strings, same 60-day window, same daily job: re-check when due, string still there → another 60 days, gone → lapses; do not fetch early; do not verify every URL). If they can edit the site’s code, the website is verified. A hidden element, an HTML comment, a script, or a response shown only to our checker still counts. The string does not have to be visible to other visitors. The badge means they control the host. A street address and any other link stay unverified. Showing the website on the public page is optional. Hiding it does not unbind it.

    **Website URL:** For a shop website verification URL, the URL must be https. http is rejected. A shop may enter an IP as their website. If it is https, the final host is that same IP, and the page has the votemap tag, accept it. The host they entered and the final response host must be the same host. A redirect that changes the host fails, including an IP that redirects to a hostname, and a hostname that redirects to a different host. A redirect that stays on that same host may pass. The fetcher (item 21) fetches that URL on save only after the client check succeeded, and again when the 60 days are up. That 60-day check is server-only. The shop only learns whether the votemap tag was there. **Solved v0 (2026-10-19), replaces the earlier rule:** a URL whose host is, or resolves to, a non-public address is rejected and never fetched: loopback, private (RFC 1918), link-local (including cloud metadata `169.254.169.254`), CGNAT, multicast, reserved, and the IPv6 equivalents. The fetcher resolves DNS once, checks every resolved address, and connects only to that checked address (no re-resolve, so DNS rebinding fails). Each redirect hop is checked the same way. Port 443 only, capped body size and timeouts. An IP website must be a public IP.

    **v0 bind is bio only, all four, including personal IG.** They put **`votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle`** in that account’s **bio**, paste the social profile URL here. We check that profile’s bio while they are logged in as that votemap account. The bio must contain `votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle` as a whole token. Handle characters are letters, digits, and underscore. The match fails if another handle character is glued on the end (`@ed` inside `@edward`, `@ed2`, `votemap.net/edward`). Text before it, and a non-handle character after it, is fine. Case-sensitive. Same rule for both strings. Before the fetch, the pasted profile URL host must be an allowlisted host for that network (`instagram.com`, `x.com`, `twitter.com`, `threads.net`, `tiktok.com`; `x.com` and `twitter.com` are the same network). After redirects, the final response URL host must still be on that same network’s allowlist. If the final host is anything else, the bind fails even when the body contains an exact token. Do not trust the URL the client says it ended on. Do not follow HTML or JavaScript redirects. A lookalike host (`instagram.com.evil.com`, punycode, userinfo like `instagram.com@evil.com`) fails. A redirect that stays on the allowlisted host may pass. **No comment on `@vote_map`. No other fallback.** Either string counts.

    **Already claimed:** A social profile URL or shop website URL that is already claimed is rejected, whether the existing claim is someone else’s or this same account. This is a validation step before a new bind. Comparison is case-insensitive. x.com and twitter.com are the same network, so the same handle on either is the same claim. A different handle, or the same handle on a different network, is not the same claim. The bio token match stays case-sensitive. Do not fold www to apex.

    **Failed tag check:** If a votemap tag check returns tag_missing, do not recheck until that user manually confirms they have fixed it. That confirmation is what allows the next check. A success still renews 60 days. retrieve_failed is unchanged: retry later the same day, do not lapse, do not wait for a manual confirm.

    **URL check rate:** An immediate check (first bind, or the user confirms they fixed a missing tag) runs on the client first, from the user’s IP. The server fetches that URL only if the client check succeeded (bio or page retrieved and the votemap tag is present). If the client returns tag_missing or retrieve_failed, the server does not fetch. A scheduled check (the 60-day job) is server-only. No client fetch. Immediate rechecks of the same URL by the same account are limited to once per 24 hours. A repeat inside that window is rejected and does not fetch.

    **Profile read:** GET the profile page. After the allowlist and final-host check, take the bio from X `meta name="twitter:description"`, Threads `meta name="description"`, Instagram `meta name="description"`, or TikTok `shareMeta.desc` in the page JSON. Decode HTML entities, then exact token match. The read returns `success` (bio text retrieved and the exact token is in it), `tag_missing` (bio text retrieved and the exact token is not in it), or `retrieve_failed` (no bio text: non-200, off-host redirect, missing field, login shell, empty body, network error). A page that has a bio without the token is `tag_missing`, never `retrieve_failed`. A login shell or a page with no bio field is `retrieve_failed`, never `tag_missing`. On a recheck, `retrieve_failed` does not lapse the bind. `tag_missing` does.

    **Daily job:** selects only binds whose 60 days are up (bio binds and a bound website). It re-fetches those only. String still there → verified for another 60 days, no user action. `tag_missing` lapses the bind. `retrieve_failed` does not. It does **not** scan every account, it does **not** re-fetch early, and it does **not** verify every URL. The bio rechecks have until end of day: spread the due binds across the day, do not fire them in a tight loop, retry `retrieve_failed` later the same day instead of immediately, and do not send another request to the same host until the previous one to that host has finished and a gap has passed.

    **60-day lag (solved v0):** After a check, the bind stays verified until the next check even if they remove the string or sell the account. Pay still goes to the wallet on that bind. The daily job does not re-fetch early. When the 60 days are up, string gone → bind lapses. This lag is accepted, not an open question ([accepted-risks.md](./accepted-risks.md)). It applies to bio binds and to the website check.

    **No social OAuth. No cold-DM. No paid X API.** X API is **not used**. FCFS votemap name is still theirs; squat-the-real-social still needs this bio. **One social @ binds to one votemap account.** **Pay picker** = verified IG / X / Threads / TikTok @ (not votemap @). Reverse lookup: social → votemap account → claimed wallet. Unbound / unverified social **cannot** be a pay dest. **Confirm before send:** social @, muted votemap @, wallet, Coinbase country. Solved v0: The phone shows the verified social @ the user picked, the dollar amount the user entered, and the claimed wallet on its own screen, and those displayed values are what get signed. The signer looks up verified social @ → votemap account → claimed wallet. The page does not supply the payee or the wallet. Web JS is untrusted. A token checked only by the page does not count. Pay is final. **Chain dest** = that claimed wallet. **UI (cards / pay):** **primary = verified social @** of that pot’s network. Votemap handle muted. No bind → votemap handle only (**not** fake-verified) — and they cannot be picked as pay dest until bound.

## Wallets and money

7. User **maps at most 3 wallets** they control (sign a message per address). One is the **payout** address. Unmapped addresses stay on this account’s pay deny-list (tombstone).
8. **Pledge / cancel / pay** needs a linked wallet, Coinbase **US** on that wallet, and the **phone app** after biometrics. Desktop/web cannot obtain a votemap signature. Readonly accounts browse only.
9. **Pledge.** The voter’s wallet signs an EIP-712 pledge: pot, amount (**≥ 1 USDC**), expiry (a date the voter picks, at most 365 days out), nonce. It is off-chain: no gas, no USDC moves. The signer checks the wallet’s Coinbase US badge on Base, stores the signed pledge, and publishes it. Anyone can verify the wallet signed it; VoteMap cannot forge one. One open pledge per (wallet, pot). A new pledge on the same pot replaces it (raise, lower, extend). **Cancel** is a signed message too, anytime, free. At expiry the pledge lapses on its own. A pledge never moves money and never obliges a transfer the contract can enforce. Its weight is reputation (item 10).
    - **Pay.** The voter’s wallet sends one transaction to the **PayRouter** on Base: pot, payee wallet, amount, optional product, votemap signature (item 19). The router moves USDC **from the sender’s wallet** (EIP-2612 `permit` in the same transaction, no standing allowance needed): **1.5%** (`floor(amount * 15 / 1000)`) to the treasury, the rest to the payee, in that one transaction. Any amount ≥ 1 USDC, any number of pays, any time. A pay reduces that wallet’s open pledge on that pot. A pay with no pledge is still a pay (a tip toward that pot). There is no vest and no clawback: the voter can pay in milestones by sending several pays.
    - **Payee.** **Picker:** verified social @ (not votemap @). Reverse lookup social → votemap account → that account’s payout wallet. Unbound / unverified social cannot be picked. **Confirm screen** before send: social @, muted votemap @, wallet, Coinbase country. The payout wallet must have been mapped for at least 24 hours. `to != msg.sender`. The signer refuses this account’s own mapped/tombstoned wallets. The router checks a **live** Coinbase US badge on the payee wallet too, so a revoked payee cannot be paid.
    - **Exactly one payee per pay.** The router never loops over payees and never holds a balance between calls. If one transfer reverts (for example a Circle-blacklisted payee), only that pay fails. Nothing can be stuck.
    - **Shop:** a pay to a merchant may name a listed product. The signed pay carries `productId` and the price. Payee must be a **merchant** account. **Register:** Coinbase US on the payout wallet + merchant flag on the account (off-chain). Must have an **address, a website, or both**. Street address is self-attested, shown **unverified**. Website verification is item 6. Does **not** prove they control the street address. Solved v0: A shop cannot be paid as a shop unless both are true: it has at least one verified link, and it has zero unverified links. A shop with no verified links is ineligible. A shop with any unverified link is ineligible. To become eligible it adds a verified link and removes every unverified link. Verified links are only TikTok, X, Threads, and Instagram via the bio rule, and a website only when that site shows `votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle`. Any other URL is an unverified link. A street address is not a link. It does not by itself disqualify a shop. An address-only shop has zero verified links, so it is ineligible. The public payee picker is unchanged. Catalog of products they sell: item 6. A shop pledge is the pledge-to-purchase case: the voter pledges toward the post’s outcome and pays the shop when they buy. Nothing is held in between. We do **not** police whether a coffee was handed over. Where that USDC goes: [flows](./flows.md#where-shop-usdc-goes).
10. **Incentives (no escrow).**
    - **Fees:** **1.5% on pay. 0% on pledge, cancel, and lapse.** No other fee. VoteMap earns only when someone gets paid. There is no exit fee, so there is nothing to dodge by paying a friend.
    - **Backed amount:** a pot shows **pledged** and **backed** USDC. A wallet’s pledges are backed only up to its current USDC balance on Base. If a wallet’s open pledges add up to more than it holds, each of its pledges is backed pro rata (`pledge × balance / total open pledges`). A wallet whose Coinbase badge is revoked backs $0. Read from chain by the indexer (item 26), never trusted from the client. Browse sorts and filters on backed, not pledged.
    - **Claims:** a verified-bind account can post a **claim** on a pot (“I did this”, a link, short text). Off-chain, public. Pledgers pick payees from claims or by social @.
    - **Follow-through:** every account shows paid ÷ pledged over its **ended** pledges (cancelled, lapsed, or fully paid) on pots where **another** account paid a claimant while that pledge was open. A pledge on a pot where nobody else paid anyone does not count against you: the outcome may not have happened. Shown on the public page and next to each pledge. Visible only: no fee discount, rank prize, or new fee.
    - Pledging is a promise to the public, not to VoteMap. VoteMap never chases, fines, or collects an unpaid pledge.
11. **PayRouter.** One immutable contract on **Base**. It holds no USDC and no per-user balances. It stores only used nonces. It emits `Paid(potId, from, to, amount, fee, productId, fromCountry)`. `potId` = `keccak256` of the canonical URL (item 23). No new contract per issue. A new rule ships as a new router address. Users lose nothing in the switch, because the old router holds nothing. Any USDC sent straight to the router is not anyone’s balance and the treasury can sweep it.

## Country (Coinbase, not a form field)

12. Coinbase **Verified Country** = their **KYC residence** (ID, sometimes proof of address). Not whatever they type in votemap. Coinbase does **not** attest legal name. **v0 money path is Coinbase-only:** pledge and pay require EAS on the sending wallet, and pay also requires it on the payee wallet. No other KYC vendor. VoteMap does not run its own sanctions list. Coinbase screens those customers. That is the v0 rule. VoteMap does not re-run the list; a delay on Coinbase’s side is an [accepted risk](./accepted-risks.md). VoteMap still must not knowingly pay a sanctioned person. Browse without Coinbase is fine (readonly). Same-person duplicate Coinbase accounts are **Coinbase’s** problem (one personal account per ID + phone).

    **How MetaMask / hardware fits:** we do **not** take USDC from a Coinbase custodial account, and we do not take it at all. The user keeps a self-custody wallet (MetaMask, Ledger, Coinbase Wallet app, etc.). They must have a **Coinbase.com** account (ID + phone), then **Onchain Verify** that wallet so Coinbase writes an EAS badge on Base: “this address is a KYC’d Coinbase user, country = US.” The PayRouter reads that badge on `msg.sender` and on `to`. No badge / not US → pay reverts. The signer reads the same badge before it accepts a pledge. That is how we get **location**. Coinbase never holds that seed.

    **Not Coinbase SSO.** Login stays Google/Apple. SSO would not put country in bytecode (Remix could skip it). Do not use OAuth country as the allowlist.
13. **The PayRouter reads EAS for `msg.sender` and `to`** (Coinbase attester, not revoked). **No `country` argument.** Passing `"US"` in calldata is a **security bug**.
14. **MVP: only `US` may pledge, pay, or be paid.** On-chain ISO **allowlist** in the router, starts with `US`. A new allowlist ships as a new router (item 11). App follows the same list. Browse can be global.
15. **Emit the payer’s ISO code** in `Paid`. The indexer keeps it after revoke so we can still see which countries paid a bounty. Do not re-query live EAS to label old money. A pledge stores the country the signer read when it accepted the pledge.

## Phone-only vote (JS is untrusted)

16. **Web / desktop / other browsers = readonly.** Browse, profiles, QR / share sheet / iOS Share to open that issue on the **phone app**. No pledge, cancel, or pay from the website.
17. **Sign / pay only on iOS or Android** after **strong biometrics** (Face ID / Class 3 fingerprint). **No PIN/passcode fallback.** No biometrics enrolled → cannot sign.
18. **Do not trust a JS `sendOk()`.** The Pay **button can be HTML** in the WebView; it may only call a native plugin (`VoteMap.pay(...)`). That call is “please start a pay,” not “biometrics passed.” Native does Face ID, signs with the enclave/keystore, and **HTTPS to the signer**. The signer never accepts an “okay” from the WebView. Console can invoke the plugin; it still cannot skip Face ID or fake the hardware signature.
19. After that device proof, **votemap EIP-712-signs the pay** (solved v0, 2026-10-19, replaces the pre-fix rule that had no nonce or deadline). Domain: name, version, **`chainId`**, **`verifyingContract`** (that router). Signed struct: **`payer`** (must equal `msg.sender`), `potId`, `to`, `amount`, `productId` (zero if none), `price` (zero if none), **`nonce`** (per payer, the router marks it used), **`deadline`** (a few minutes; the router rejects it after). Every economic field the phone showed is in the struct. A signature works once, on one chain, on one router, from one wallet. Remix without it reverts. Because the payer’s own wallet must send it, a stolen votemap key cannot move anyone else’s USDC. It can only skip votemap’s rules on the thief’s own pays.
20. **Nothing to freeze.** VoteMap holds no USDC, so VoteMap being down cannot trap anyone’s money. While the signer is down, pays are paused and pledges stay as they are. A voter can always send USDC directly outside votemap; it just does not count as a votemap pay.
21. **Signing key in KMS / HSM, never in env or git** (solved v0, 2026-10-19). The signing service only signs. It holds no other secrets and makes no outbound fetches. All outbound fetches (bio, website, oEmbed snapshot) run in a separate **fetcher** with no secrets and no route to private networks (item 6). Owner can rotate the key: a new key is a new signer address that the router accepts by router version (item 11). This is **not** the country check. Cheap path: thin native shell (Capacitor/plugin) around the same UI — **not** React Native. Native plugin does biometrics + keystore; web JS does not.

## Issues (posts)

22. **Paste / embed must be a public canonical permalink.** Reject private / non-embeddable / wrong host in the **app and signer**. A post that is private, deleted, or not embeddable **at first pledge** does not get through. **No pot.**
    - **X:** `x.com/{user}/status/{id}` (also `twitter.com`). Keep `{user}` for snapshot. Pot key may be `x.com/i/status/{id}` so pots don’t split.
    - **Threads:** `threads.net/@{user}/post/{id}` (also `threads.com`)
    - **Instagram:** `instagram.com/p/{code}` or `/reel/{code}` (or `/{user}/p/` — any content URL, not Reels-only)
    - **TikTok:** `tiktok.com/@{user}/video/{id}` — resolve `vm.` / `vt.` first, **never** key on short links
23. **Signer/app canonicalize before signing:** `twitter.com` → `x.com`, strip `www.`, strip tracking query/fragment, resolve `vm.`/`vt.` to permalink, no trailing slash junk. Pot key = that canonical string; `potId` = its `keccak256`. Reject if resolve fails. The router only sees `potId` (bytecode cannot HTTP-fetch). **Why not scrape the live embed:** official widgets become **cross-origin iframes after hydrate** — we cannot read handle / date / caption from them. **Snapshot at first pledge** for the browse index (item 25). Issue page may still render the embed. No categories (group by URL).
24. No mock chain, no placeholder treasury. Treasury **required at deploy**. `/mvp` errors until chain + router env are set.
25. **Browse index is v0 (solved).** First pledge: the **fetcher** fetches a snapshot from **exact-host-allowlisted** oEmbed / permalink hosts. **Never trust client-supplied handle / text / date.** Store one row: `network`, `handle`, `postedAt`, pot hash, one-line text. Browse = paged queries on that index (`LIMIT 50`). **Do not refetch 1M posts.** Pot key = canonical URL. Changing the URL = a **different pot**; cannot overwrite another post’s row. Resolve TikTok `vm.`/`vt.` once, then key the permalink. A **malicious URL is solvable this way** (exact host, fetcher, one row per pot key) — not an open cheat. Live embed **only** on the issue page (filters never read it).

## What we write where

**Nothing personal goes on-chain** (solved v0, 2026-10-19). On-chain data is public and permanent, so it can never be deleted. On-chain is only: wallet addresses, `potId`, amounts, fee, `productId`, the payer’s ISO country (already public in Coinbase’s EAS badge), and used nonces. No email, name, OIDC `sub`, gender, birth year, phone, or any hash of them. A hash of a phone number is not private: the number space is small enough to brute-force.

| Gender, birth year | Yes (self-reported) | **Never** |

| Country | Preview from EAS | EAS lookup at pay; payer’s code emitted in `Paid` |

## PayRouter must revert (not UI-only)

- pay < 1 USDC  
- no live Coinbase EAS US badge on `msg.sender`, or on `to`; country not on the allowlist  
- missing or bad votemap EIP-712 sig; `payer != msg.sender`; nonce already used; past `deadline`; wrong `chainId` or router  
- `to == msg.sender`  
- fee: `floor(amount * 15 / 1000)` to the treasury, rest to `to`, both from `msg.sender` in the same call; nothing kept in the router  
- one `to` per call; no loops over payees  

**Cannot live in bytecode:** Google/Apple session, SMS, unique E.164, votemap handle, Face ID, same-account mapped-wallet graph, 24-hour payout-wallet age, HTTP canonicalize/oEmbed, shop address/website, merchant eligibility, **shop products**, public page links, pledges, claims, backed amount, follow-through, **browse index**, **social → votemap → wallet payee lookup**, public-official block (item 27), **named trail** (wallet → votemap username → bound socials). Direct Remix skips biometrics **and** has no votemap sig, so a pay through the router reverts. Users can still send USDC to anyone directly; that is their own transfer and is not a votemap pay.

## Site / cost

- New UI in `mvp/`. Landing page untouched. Static export. Portable **Google/Apple OIDC** + SMS + signer HTTP (not Cloudflare-only). **X API not used.** Browse index lives on the **API** (or tiny read API), not in the export.
- Three services: **API** (sessions, profiles, pledges, index, trail), **signer** (KMS key, signs only), **fetcher** (outbound fetches only, item 6). Plus an **indexer** for `Paid` events and pledger balances (item 26).
- SMS OTP costs us per code. Users pay Base gas on pay only; pledge and cancel cost no gas.

## Where the money went

26. **v0:** he can see where the money went. The trail reads as votemap names — **`@isaac` paid `@grok`** — not only tx hashes. Map **wallet → votemap username**, and through that username to **bound Instagram / X / Threads / TikTok**. **Basescan** stays as the raw link for pays. No privacy hide.

    **Minimal (least to operate):** rows come from two places. **Pledge** and **cancel** rows come from the stored wallet-signed messages. **Pay** rows come from the router’s `Paid` events, so a row exists only after the transaction is on Base; a reverted pay never shows. ## Who cannot be paid

27. **No payouts to public officials** (solved v0, 2026-10-19). A payment to an official for an official act is bribery or an illegal gratuity, and a payment to a candidate can be a campaign contribution. These cannot be paid, through any account, shop, or wallet they control: a sitting or elected public official (federal, state, or local), a candidate for public office, their staff, and their immediate family.
    - **Payee attestation:** before an account can be picked as a payee for the first time, it attests that it is none of these and is not being paid for an official act. Stored with a timestamp. A false attestation is the payee’s act, and the account is blocked when found.
    - **Official handle list:** the signer refuses to sign a pay to any account with a bound social on the officials list. The list is built from free public data (the `unitedstates/congress-legislators` dataset for Congress, Open States for state legislators) and refreshed on a schedule. A bind to a listed handle still verifies, so the official can pledge and pay, but cannot be paid.
    - **No paying for votes:** a pot whose post offers money for voting a certain way, or for voting at all, is refused at first pledge, and the signer refuses pays on it if found later.
    - This answers PEP screening for v0: **payees** are screened against officials; **payers** are not.
