# votemap v0 requirements

Open questions: [open.md](./open.md). Known accepted risks: [accepted-risks.md](./accepted-risks.md). Later (not solved, unfinished): [later.md](./later.md).

Locked with Isaac, 2026-09-26 (credits + vest ≤365d 2026-09-28; bio bind 2026-10-02; hybrid bind 2026-10-19; shop place + public page 2026-10-02; named money trail 2026-10-03; shop catalog 2026-10-03; redeem is final 2026-10-03; legal name not collected, website verification, and the 60-day lag 2026-10-03; outcome payee and OFAC via Coinbase 2026-10-03; shop bounty eligibility 2026-10-09; exact bio token, bio URL host, and cloaked website string 2026-10-09; profile bio read and paced daily job 2026-10-09; secure phone confirm 2026-10-09; shop website URL https and same host 2026-10-09; already claimed and failed tag check 2026-10-09; client-then-server URL check and 24-hour same-URL limit 2026-10-09; no custody, pledge and direct pay, no PII on-chain, isolated fetcher, signature replay fix, one payee per pay, and no payouts to public officials 2026-10-19). This is the **solved v0** page. Production: [PR #102](https://github.com/isaacy13/votemap.net/pull/102). [PR #101](https://github.com/isaacy13/votemap.net/pull/101) is **stopped**. Diagrams: [flows](./flows.md). The user-flow and system-flow PNGs are not in this repo. Cheats that are locked: [cheats-and-fixes.md](./cheats-and-fixes.md).

**Vote** = pledge USDC on an **X, Threads, Instagram, or TikTok** post, then pay the person who did the work straight from your own wallet. Pledges are for an outcome. The reward goes to the person who did the work (builder, writer, shop, advocate). v0 does not file lobbying disclosures (LDA or otherwise). No lobbying-filing product. A public official, a candidate, their staff or immediate family, or a wallet or shop they control, cannot be paid (item 27).

**No custody (solved v0, 2026-10-19).** VoteMap never holds USDC. There is no factory balance, no escrow, no shop credit, and no vest. USDC stays in the voter’s wallet until that voter pays. A pay moves USDC from the voter’s wallet straight to the payee’s wallet in the voter’s own transaction. “Pledge” means a public, wallet-signed promise to pay up to an amount toward a post’s outcome before a date. It is not a deposit, not DeFi / yield / validator staking, and not a contract balance. Pay is final: VoteMap cannot refund, reverse, or freeze it, because VoteMap never had it.

**Incentives (solved v0).** Without escrow the pledge is the signal and reputation is the commitment. Item 10 has the rules. In short: pledging and cancelling are free, so signalling is cheap; a pot shows **backed** dollars, never more than pledgers actually hold; every account shows its **follow-through** (once someone claimed the work, did it pay someone, decline with a reason, or go silent); each pledger judges the outcome alone, so no pot-level vote exists for a whale to control; no percentage steers anyone: round-trip detection keeps the numbers hard to fake, and pays carry no fee (an optional tip to votemap at most).

## Account (Google / Apple)

1. Sign in with **Google** or **Apple** (Isaac already pays Apple Developer).
2. Save OIDC **`sub`**, **email**, **name**. Key by `sub`, not email. Apple **name only on first Allow** — must save then. Email may be Hide My Email. **Legal name is not collected and is not a signup field.** v0 does not require a legal name to sign up. The OIDC `name` is the provider display name, not a legal name.
3. Then the votemap **handle**. **No phone and no SMS OTP** (removed 2026-10-19): every pledge, pay, and payee already needs a Coinbase-KYC’d wallet (one Coinbase account per ID, at most 3 verified wallets), so a phone number added little and cost money and SMS-pumping risk. One KYC’d person gets **one** votemap account (item 5, Sign in with Coinbase, 2026-10-22).
    - **Optional demographics (analytics only):** **gender** and **birth year**, self-reported, unverified, with “prefer not to say”. Asked after the first pledge, never a signup gate. Off-chain only, never on the public page, never per account in any export. Shown only as aggregates over at least 20 accounts per group, so nobody can be singled out. Google/Apple do **not** provide gender/age.
4. Unique votemap **handle** (first-come). One Google/Apple `sub` → one account. **A votemap handle cannot be changed or released** (2026-10-19), so old trail rows always name the account that acted. Handles are **FCFS**. Squat-the-name is **v0-ok** (votemap `@nike` is theirs). It does **not** confer Instagram `@nike`. **Pay picker is verified social @, not votemap @.**
5. **Wallet is optional.** No wallet = **readonly** (browse, edit own profile). Cannot post, pledge, or pay until a wallet is linked.
    - **Sign in with Coinbase, once, before the first wallet (2026-10-22).** Coinbase App OAuth2 with the read-only `wallet:user:read` scope; the API reads the Coinbase user `id` from `/v2/user` and stores only a keyed hash (HMAC) of it, then drops the token. **One Coinbase user → one votemap account.** Coinbase allows one personal account per ID, so one person cannot run two votemap accounts and pay themselves. A wallet’s Coinbase badge cannot be tied to that sign-in (EAS does not say which Coinbase account attested it), so mapping a wallet attested under a friend’s Coinbase account still works; that is the friend case. **Lost Google/Apple login:** Sign in with Coinbase on a new login recovers the existing account (same 72-hour wait and email notice as re-enroll, item 17); it never creates a second one. One account can pledge, claim, and be a shop; a person running two shops is later (Coinbase Business). Before build: confirm the `id` is stable and OAuth app terms allow this use. If Coinbase refuses the OAuth app, v0 still ships with the other links below and self pays drop back to a bounded risk.
6. **Public page** at **`votemap.net/@username`** (votemap handle). Linktree-like. They customize which links show (labels, order, what is listed). They can **show their shop** on this page as well as links. **v0 shop is a catalog**, not only an address on the profile. Shops list products they sell. A product can be physical or not. Each listed product: **name**, **price in USDC**, **physical or not**, **short text**. Off-chain, like links. Not a token. Not shipping labels, tax, themes, inventory sync, or staff accounts. License stays **later**. Hiding a social on this page does **not** unbind it. Pay picker still uses the bio bind. Token text stays **`votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle`**. This page is the public URL. Paying a shop: item 9.

    **Verified marks (v0):** only TikTok, X, Threads, and Instagram, via the bind rule (X: a post carrying `votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle`; TikTok and Threads: OAuth; Instagram: that token in the bio; 60 days, daily job on due binds only), and a website only if that site’s owner puts `votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle` in its DNS TXT record or `/.well-known/votemap.txt` (requirements item 6) (same two strings, same 60-day window, same daily job: re-check when due, string still there → another 60 days, gone → lapses; do not fetch early; do not verify every URL). **Where the string must be (2026-10-22, replaces “anywhere on the page”):** only in a place the host’s owner controls, never in page content. Either a DNS TXT record at `_votemap.<host>`, or the file `https://<host>/.well-known/votemap.txt`. Either one passes. A string anywhere else (a page body, a post, a comment, a hidden element) does not count, because a post on x.com or a comment on someone’s blog would otherwise verify x.com or that blog. The verified link is the **host only**, never a path, and the badge means they control that exact host (`shop.example.com` does not verify `example.com`). An IP website uses the file, since it has no DNS. A street address and any other link stay unverified. Showing the website on the public page is optional. Hiding it does not unbind it.

    **Website URL:** For a shop website verification URL, the URL must be https. The host they enter is what gets checked; any path they type is dropped. http is rejected. A shop may enter an IP as their website. If it is https, the final host is that same IP, and the page has the votemap tag, accept it. The host they entered and the final response host must be the same host. A redirect that changes the host fails, including an IP that redirects to a hostname, and a hostname that redirects to a different host. A redirect that stays on that same host may pass. The fetcher (item 21) checks that host’s TXT record and `/.well-known/votemap.txt` on save, and again when the 60 days are up. That 60-day check is server-only. The shop only learns whether the votemap tag was there. **Solved v0 (2026-10-19), replaces the earlier rule:** a URL whose host is, or resolves to, a non-public address is rejected and never fetched: loopback, private (RFC 1918), link-local (including cloud metadata `169.254.169.254`), CGNAT, multicast, reserved, and the IPv6 equivalents. The fetcher resolves DNS once, checks every resolved address, and connects only to that checked address (no re-resolve, so DNS rebinding fails). Each redirect hop is checked the same way. Port 443 only, capped body size and timeouts. An IP website must be a public IP.

    **v0 bind method per network (hybrid, solved v0 2026-10-19; replaces bio-only for X, TikTok, and Threads).** Free official endpoints wherever they exist; scraping only where nothing else is free.
    - **X: post a token.** They publish a post whose text contains **`votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle`**, and paste that post’s canonical permalink here. The fetcher reads it from X’s free oEmbed endpoint (`publish.x.com/oembed`, exact host, no token). The bind is for the post’s **author** as oEmbed reports it (`author_url`), never a handle the client typed. (Sign in with X would also work but reading the signed-in profile is a paid X API call, about $0.01 each in 2026; it stays the fallback if oEmbed breaks.) The post must stay up: the 60-day recheck reads the same post again.
    - **TikTok: Login Kit (2026-10-20).** They sign in with TikTok Login Kit (free). Scopes: `user.info.basic` (stable `open_id`) and `user.info.profile` (the @username; free, needs TikTok pre-approval). Read only, no posting scopes. The API stores `open_id`, the username, and the refresh token, encrypted with a KMS key. The 60-day recheck refreshes the token and reads the user info. No votemap tag is needed for TikTok.
    - **Threads: OAuth.** They sign in with the Threads API (`threads_basic`, Meta app review required). The API stores the returned user id and username and the long-lived token, encrypted with a KMS key. The 60-day recheck calls the Threads `/me` endpoint with that token, refreshed as Threads requires. No votemap tag is needed for Threads.
    - **Instagram: bio.** They put the token in the account’s **bio** (personal accounts included) and paste the profile URL. The fetcher reads the bio (Profile read below). Instagram has no free official path for personal accounts, so this is the one scraped network, and the 7-day unverifiable ceiling is its safety net.

    **Token and URL rules (X, TikTok post text; Instagram bio).** We check while they are logged in as that votemap account. The text must contain `votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle` as a whole token. Handle characters are letters, digits, and underscore. The match fails if another handle character is glued on the end (`@ed` inside `@edward`, `@ed2`, `votemap.net/edward`). Text before it, and a non-handle character after it, is fine. Case-sensitive. Same rule for both strings. Before the fetch, the pasted post or profile URL host must be an allowlisted host for that network (`instagram.com`, `x.com`, `twitter.com`, `threads.net`, `tiktok.com`; `x.com` and `twitter.com` are the same network). After redirects, the final response URL host must still be on that same network’s allowlist. If the final host is anything else, the bind fails even when the body contains an exact token. Do not trust the URL the client says it ended on. Do not follow HTML or JavaScript redirects. A lookalike host (`instagram.com.evil.com`, punycode, userinfo like `instagram.com@evil.com`) fails. A redirect that stays on the allowlisted host may pass. **No comment on `@vote_map`. No other fallback.** Either string counts.

    **Already claimed:** A social profile URL or shop website URL that already has a **verified** bind is rejected, whether that bind is someone else’s or this same account. A pending bind (never verified, or waiting on a manual confirm after `tag_missing`) does not block anyone: whoever verifies first gets the bind, and the other pending binds on that URL are dropped (2026-10-19). For X the claim is the oEmbed author’s account, not the post URL, and for TikTok and Threads it is the OAuth user id. This is a validation step before a new bind. Comparison is case-insensitive. x.com and twitter.com are the same network, so the same handle on either is the same claim. A different handle, or the same handle on a different network, is not the same claim. The bio token match stays case-sensitive. Do not fold www to apex.

    **Failed tag check:** If a votemap tag check returns tag_missing, do not recheck until that user manually confirms they have fixed it. That confirmation is what allows the next check. A success still renews 60 days. retrieve_failed is unchanged: retry later the same day, do not lapse, do not wait for a manual confirm.

    **URL check rate:** Every check is server-side, in the fetcher (2026-10-19; replaces the client-first check, which browser JS cannot do because of CORS). An immediate check is a first bind, or the user confirming they fixed a missing tag. Immediate rechecks of the same URL by the same account are limited to once per 24 hours. A repeat inside that window is rejected and does not fetch. Immediate checks are also rate-limited per account, per IP, and per target host, and share the daily job’s per-host pacing.

    **Reads and results.** Instagram profile read: GET the profile page; after the allowlist and final-host check, take the bio from `meta name="description"`. X post read: GET the oEmbed JSON; take the author and the tweet text in `html`. Decode HTML entities, then exact token match. Threads and TikTok: read the signed-in user with the stored token. Every read returns `success` (text retrieved and the exact token is in it; Threads / TikTok: the token returns the bound user id), `tag_missing` (text retrieved and the token is not in it; the post is deleted or private (oEmbed 404); the oEmbed author is not the bound account; Threads / TikTok: token revoked or a different user id), or `retrieve_failed` (non-200 other than 404, off-host redirect, missing field, login shell, empty body, rate limit, network error). A bio or post with text but no token is `tag_missing`, never `retrieve_failed`. A login shell or a page with no bio field is `retrieve_failed`, never `tag_missing`. On a recheck, `retrieve_failed` does not lapse the bind. `tag_missing` does. **Unverifiable ceiling (2026-10-19):** if a due bind gets only `retrieve_failed` for **7 days in a row**, it becomes **unverifiable**: still bound, still shown, but not marked verified and **cannot be picked as a payee** until a check succeeds. A success restores verified for another 60 days. Seven days covers ordinary outages and short blocks; past that, a bind nobody can read must stop receiving money (the longest a bind can go unread and still be paid is 67 days).

    **Daily job:** selects only binds whose 60 days are up (social binds and a bound website). It re-fetches those only. String still there → verified for another 60 days, no user action. `tag_missing` lapses the bind. `retrieve_failed` does not, until the 7-day unverifiable ceiling. It does **not** scan every account, it does **not** re-fetch early, and it does **not** verify every URL. The rechecks have until end of day: spread the due binds across the day, do not fire them in a tight loop, retry `retrieve_failed` later the same day instead of immediately, and do not send another request to the same host until the previous one to that host has finished and a gap has passed.

    **60-day lag (solved v0):** After a check, the bind stays verified until the next check even if they remove the string or sell the account. Pay still goes to the wallet on that bind. The daily job does not re-fetch early. When the 60 days are up, string gone → bind lapses. This lag is accepted, not an open question ([accepted-risks.md](./accepted-risks.md)). It applies to bio binds and to the website check.

    **No social OAuth except Threads and TikTok (read-only scopes). No cold-DM. No paid X API.** The paid X API is **not used**; X oEmbed is free and keyless. FCFS votemap name is still theirs; squat-the-real-social still needs this bind. **One social @ binds to one votemap account.** **Pay picker** = verified IG / X / Threads / TikTok @ (not votemap @). Reverse lookup: social → votemap account → claimed wallet. Unbound / unverified social **cannot** be a pay dest. **Confirm before send:** social @, muted votemap @, wallet, Coinbase country. Solved v0: The phone shows the verified social @ the user picked, the dollar amount the user entered, and the claimed wallet on its own screen, and those displayed values are what get signed. The signer looks up verified social @ → votemap account → claimed wallet. The page does not supply the payee or the wallet. Web JS is untrusted. A token checked only by the page does not count. Pay is final. **Chain dest** = that claimed wallet. **UI (cards / pay):** **primary = verified social @** of that pot’s network. Votemap handle muted. No bind → votemap handle only (**not** fake-verified) — and they cannot be picked as pay dest until bound.

## Wallets and money

7. User **maps at most 3 wallets** they control (sign a message per address). **Smart wallets count** (2026-10-19): a wallet’s signature is checked with ERC-1271, or ERC-6492 for a smart wallet not yet deployed, so a passkey smart wallet such as Coinbase Smart Wallet can be mapped, pledge, pay, and be the payout. That removes the seed phrase and browser extension from signup. Whether Coinbase Onchain Verify attests a smart-wallet address is **not yet confirmed**: Coinbase’s docs say any self-custodied EVM address and list Coinbase Wallet, but never name smart wallets, so test it with one real account before relying on it.
    - **Payout change and new wallets (solved v0, 2026-10-19).** Mapping a new wallet or changing the payout wallet needs a fresh biometric on the enrolled phone (item 17), plus one of: a signature from the **current payout wallet** (takes effect at once), or a **72-hour wait** with an email to the account that can cancel it. A stolen Google or Apple login alone cannot redirect payouts. A newly mapped wallet cannot be the payout until it is mapped and past any wait. One is the **payout** address. Unmapped addresses stay on this account’s pay deny-list (tombstone).
8. **Pledge / cancel / pay** needs a linked wallet, Coinbase **US** on that wallet, and the **phone app** after biometrics. Desktop/web cannot obtain a votemap signature. Readonly accounts browse only.
9. **Pledge.** The voter’s wallet signs an EIP-712 pledge: pot, amount (**≥ 1 USDC**), expiry (a date the voter picks, at most 365 days out), nonce. It is off-chain: no gas, no USDC moves. The signer checks the wallet’s Coinbase US badge on Base, stores the signed pledge, and publishes it. Anyone can verify the wallet signed it; VoteMap cannot forge one. One open pledge per (wallet, pot). A new pledge on the same pot replaces it (raise, lower, extend). **Cancel** is a signed message too, anytime, free. At expiry the pledge lapses on its own. A pledge never moves money and never obliges a transfer the contract can enforce. Its weight is reputation (item 10).
    - **Pay.** The voter’s wallet sends one transaction to the **PayRouter** on Base: pot, payee wallet, amount, optional product, votemap signature (item 19). The router moves USDC **from the sender’s wallet** (EIP-2612 `permit` in the same transaction, no standing allowance needed): the full amount to the payee, plus the payer’s optional tip to the treasury, in that one transaction. Any amount ≥ 1 USDC, any number of pays, any time. A pay reduces that wallet’s open pledge on that pot. A pay with no pledge is still a pay (a tip toward that pot). There is no vest and no clawback: the voter can pay in milestones by sending several pays.
    - **Payee.** **Picker:** verified social @ (not votemap @). Any verified social on the payee’s account can be picked, not only the pot’s network; the pot’s network is listed first (2026-10-22). Reverse lookup social → votemap account → that account’s payout wallet. Unbound / unverified social cannot be picked. **Confirm screen** before send: social @, muted votemap @, wallet, Coinbase country. The payout wallet must have been mapped for at least 24 hours, and any payout change must be past its wait (item 7). `to != msg.sender`. The signer refuses this account’s own mapped/tombstoned wallets. The router checks a **live** Coinbase US badge on the payee wallet too, so a revoked payee cannot be paid.
    - **Exactly one payee per pay.** The router never loops over payees and never holds a balance between calls. If one transfer reverts (for example a Circle-blacklisted payee), only that pay fails. Nothing can be stuck.
    - **Shop:** a pay to a merchant may name a listed product. The signed pay carries `productId` and the price. The signer refuses a pay whose price is not that product’s current catalog price (2026-10-22). Payee must be a **merchant** account. **Register:** Coinbase US on the payout wallet + merchant flag on the account (off-chain). Must have an **address, a website, or both**. Street address is self-attested, shown **unverified**. Website verification is item 6. Does **not** prove they control the street address. Solved v0: A shop cannot be paid as a shop unless both are true: it has at least one verified link, and it has zero unverified links. A shop with no verified links is ineligible. A shop with any unverified link is ineligible. To become eligible it adds a verified link and removes every unverified link. Verified links are only TikTok, X, Threads, and Instagram via the bio rule, and a website only when that site’s DNS TXT record or `/.well-known/votemap.txt` has `votemap/@handle` or `votemap.net/handle`. Any other URL is an unverified link. A street address is not a link. It does not by itself disqualify a shop. An address-only shop has zero verified links, so it is ineligible. The public payee picker is unchanged. Catalog of products they sell: item 6. A shop pledge is the pledge-to-purchase case: the voter pledges toward the post’s outcome and pays the shop when they buy. Nothing is held in between. We do **not** police whether a coffee was handed over. Where that USDC goes: [flows](./flows.md#where-shop-usdc-goes).
10. **Incentives (no escrow; redesigned 2026-10-22).** No percentage is used to steer behavior: a fee charges an honest pay exactly as much as a sham one, so it cannot tell them apart. Each attack is handled by building the metric so the attack does not move it. Every abuse scenario and its verdict: [cheats-and-fixes](./cheats-and-fixes.md#incentive-scenarios).
    - **Hard limit, stated plainly:** as long as each pledger freely picks whom to pay, nothing can stop a pledger paying a friend who hands the money back in cash. No escrow, KYC, or fee changes that. The rules below make such a pay buy as little as possible and be visible.
    - **Fees:** **no fee on pledge, cancel, lapse, or pay.** At pay the payer may add an optional **tip to votemap**, shown empty (not pre-filled); the router sends it to the treasury in the same transaction. VoteMap never earns more because an outcome failed. (Pending Isaac’s pricing choice: no fee + optional tip / 1.5% on every pay / 1.5% on shop sales only.)
    - **One person, one account:** item 5 (Sign in with Coinbase). Accounts are also **linked** when they share a phone app install or device key, a Google/Apple login, or any wallet ever mapped (current or tombstoned). Linked accounts count as one person everywhere below: the signer refuses a pay between them, and a claim by a linked account never makes a pledge count. Links stay private; only the effect shows.
    - **Backed amount:** a pot shows **pledged** and **backed** USDC. A wallet’s pledges are backed only up to its USDC balance on Base. If a wallet’s open pledges add up to more than it holds, each is backed pro rata (`pledge × balance / total open pledges`). A wallet whose Coinbase badge is revoked backs $0. The indexer reads every balance **at one block** (item 26), so moving USDC between wallets mid-read cannot count it twice. Never trusted from the client. Browse sorts and filters on backed.
    - **Concentration:** each pot shows the largest single person’s share of backed, so one whale does not read as a crowd.
    - **Claims:** a verified-bind account can post one **claim** per pot (“I did this”, a link, short text), at most 20 open claims per account. Off-chain, public. Pledgers are told about new claims in one daily digest, not one alert per claim.
    - **Each pledger judges alone.** There is no pot-level verdict, vote, or “accepted” claim. Outcomes are often taste (one pledger pays the ice cream shop, another wanted gelato), and a pot-wide threshold would let whoever holds enough backed dollars decide for everyone. Paying any eligible payee keeps the pledge. No other pledger’s choice changes a pledger’s record.
    - **Claim split:** each pot shows, per claimant, the USDC paid to them, how many people paid, and their share of backed. Information only; it decides nothing. A pay to a payee with no claim on that pot is labelled **no claim**.
    - **Decline:** a pledger can decline with a preset reason (“not done”, “not what I wanted”, “paid outside votemap”, “changed my mind”) plus optional text up to 140 characters, signed like a pledge. Allowed until 30 days after the pledge ends. When a pledge ends on a pot with a claim, the app asks once: paid, or which reason. Decline text is under the stop rule (item 28).
    - **Payee context:** the confirm screen and the trail show the payee’s account age, how many people (unlinked accounts) have ever paid them, and whether they claimed that pot.
    - **Round trips:** for 30 days after a pay, the indexer checks USDC `Transfer` logs from the payee’s wallets to any wallet of the payer or a linked account (one filtered `eth_getLogs` per pay, free RPC). A returned pay is marked **returned** and counts nowhere. Hand-backs through other wallets or in cash are not detected (the hard limit above).
    - **Follow-through (each account’s record):** counts only pledges that ended on a pot that got a claim, by an account not linked to the pledger, before the pledge ended or within 30 days after. For each such pledge the **counted amount** is the pledge’s **highest amount from 30 days before that pot’s first such claim until it ended**, so cutting or cancelling a pledge just before or after the work lands still counts. That amount splits into **paid** (pays on that pot, minus returned), **declined** (rest, if a reason was given), and **silent** (rest). Record shows all three; headline is paid. Pledges on pots nobody claimed do not count. Visible only: no discount, rank prize, or fee.
    - **Expected payout:** each pot shows Σ over pledgers of backed × reliability. Reliability = `(weighted paid + k × average) / (counted amount + k)` over that account’s record, where `average` is all accounts’ paid share and **`k` = the larger of $100 and this pledge’s backed amount**. So a record only vouches at the scale it was earned: $100 of history barely moves a $10,000 pledge off the average. In **weighted paid**, a pay counts in full once its payee has been paid by at least 3 people other than the payer (any pot, any time, so it upgrades later); until then it counts half. A friend ring of four KYC’d people beats this; one friend does not.
    - **Public inputs:** signed pledges, cancels, declines, and claims are published daily with a Merkle root anchored on Base (same job as item 28), so anyone can recompute every record except the private account links.
    - Pledging is a promise to the public, not to VoteMap. VoteMap never chases, fines, or collects an unpaid pledge.
11. **PayRouter.** One immutable contract on **Base**. It holds no USDC and no per-user balances. It stores only used nonces. It emits `Paid(potId, from, to, amount, tip, productId, fromCountry)`. `potId` = `keccak256` of the canonical URL (item 23). No new contract per issue. A new rule ships as a new router address. Users lose nothing in the switch, because the old router holds nothing. Any USDC sent straight to the router is not anyone’s balance and the treasury can sweep it.

## Country (Coinbase, not a form field)

12. Coinbase **Verified Country** = their **KYC residence** (ID, sometimes proof of address). Not whatever they type in votemap. Coinbase does **not** attest legal name. **v0 money path is Coinbase-only:** pledge and pay require EAS on the sending wallet, and pay also requires it on the payee wallet. No other KYC vendor. VoteMap does not run its own sanctions list. Coinbase screens those customers. That is the v0 rule. VoteMap does not re-run the list; a delay on Coinbase’s side is an [accepted risk](./accepted-risks.md). VoteMap still must not knowingly pay a sanctioned person. Browse without Coinbase is fine (readonly). Same-person duplicate Coinbase accounts are **Coinbase’s** problem (one personal account per ID + phone).

    **How MetaMask / hardware fits:** we do **not** take USDC from a Coinbase custodial account, and we do not take it at all. The user keeps a self-custody wallet (MetaMask, Ledger, Coinbase Wallet app, etc.). They must have a **Coinbase.com** account (ID + phone), then **Onchain Verify** that wallet so Coinbase writes an EAS badge on Base: “this address is a KYC’d Coinbase user, country = US.” The PayRouter reads that badge on `msg.sender` and on `to`. No badge / not US → pay reverts. The signer reads the same badge before it accepts a pledge. That is how we get **location**. Coinbase never holds that seed.

    **Not Coinbase SSO.** Login stays Google/Apple. SSO would not put country in bytecode (Remix could skip it). Do not use OAuth country as the allowlist.
13. **The PayRouter reads EAS for `msg.sender` and `to`** (Coinbase attester, not revoked). **No `country` argument.** Passing `"US"` in calldata is a **security bug**.
14. **MVP: only `US` may pledge, pay, or be paid.** On-chain ISO **allowlist** in the router, starts with `US`. A new allowlist ships as a new router (item 11). App follows the same list. Browse can be global.
15. **Emit the payer’s ISO code** in `Paid`. The indexer keeps it after revoke so we can still see which countries paid a bounty. Do not re-query live EAS to label old money. A pledge stores the country the signer read when it accepted the pledge.

## Phone-only vote (JS is untrusted)

16. **Web / desktop / other browsers = readonly.** Browse, profiles, QR / share sheet / iOS Share to open that issue on the **phone app**. No pledge, cancel, or pay from the website.
17. **Sign / pay only on iOS or Android** after **strong biometrics** (Face ID / Class 3 fingerprint). **No PIN/passcode fallback.** No biometrics enrolled → cannot sign.

    **Biometric-only device key (solved v0, 2026-10-19).** The goal is a live human at the moment of each pledge or pay, not proof that the device is theirs. A PIN, passcode, pattern, or Windows Hello PIN does not count. Browser passkeys (WebAuthn) do not count either: their user-verification flag cannot tell a fingerprint from a PIN, which is why pledge and pay stay in the native app.
    - **Enroll:** the app creates a P-256 key in the Secure Enclave (iOS) or StrongBox / TEE Keystore (Android) that can only be used after a **strong biometric**, with **no device-credential fallback**, and that is **destroyed when biometrics change**. iOS: access control `.biometryCurrentSet`. Android: `setUserAuthenticationParameters(0, AUTH_BIOMETRIC_STRONG)` and `setInvalidatedByBiometricEnrollment(true)`. Adding a new face or finger therefore kills the key, so someone cannot add their own finger to another person’s phone.
    - **Prove it to the server at enroll:** iOS **App Attest** (genuine app on a genuine device) for that key. Android **Key Attestation** chain checked by the server: hardware-backed, biometric-strong-only auth, invalidated on enrollment, plus a **Play Integrity** verdict for app and device. Emulators, rooted or jailbroken devices, and modified apps fail enroll.
    - **Every action:** the API sends a one-time challenge bound to the exact action (pot, amount, payee wallet, product, price). The device key signs that challenge, which needs a fresh biometric each time (no reuse window). The signer issues the EIP-712 pay signature (item 19), or the API accepts the pledge, only after verifying it.
    - **Re-enroll** after a biometric change or a new phone: Google/Apple login + a signature from a mapped wallet + a 72-hour wait with an email notice to the account, then a new attested key. Old keys for that account are revoked.
    - **What this does and does not prove:** a live person passed the phone’s biometric just now. It does not prove that person is unique. Uniqueness comes from Coinbase KYC (one personal account per ID) on every money-path wallet.
18. **Do not trust a JS `sendOk()`.** The Pay **button can be HTML** in the WebView; it may only call a native plugin (`VoteMap.pay(...)`). That call is “please start a pay,” not “biometrics passed.” Native does Face ID, signs with the enclave/keystore, and **HTTPS to the signer**. The signer never accepts an “okay” from the WebView. Console can invoke the plugin; it still cannot skip Face ID or fake the hardware signature.
19. After that device proof, **votemap EIP-712-signs the pay** (solved v0, 2026-10-19, replaces the pre-fix rule that had no nonce or deadline). Domain: name, version, **`chainId`**, **`verifyingContract`** (that router). Signed struct: **`payer`** (must equal `msg.sender`), `potId`, `to`, `amount`, `tip`, `productId` (zero if none), `price` (zero if none), **`nonce`** (per payer, the router marks it used), **`deadline`** (a few minutes; the router rejects it after). Every economic field the phone showed is in the struct. A signature works once, on one chain, on one router, from one wallet. Remix without it reverts. Because the payer’s own wallet must send it, a stolen votemap key cannot move anyone else’s USDC. It can only skip votemap’s rules on the thief’s own pays.
20. **Nothing to freeze.** VoteMap holds no USDC, so VoteMap being down cannot trap anyone’s money. While the signer is down, pays are paused and pledges stay as they are. A voter can always send USDC directly outside votemap; it just does not count as a votemap pay.
21. **Signing key in KMS / HSM, never in env or git** (solved v0, 2026-10-19). The signing service only signs. It holds no other secrets and makes no outbound fetches. All outbound fetches (bio, website, oEmbed snapshot) run in a separate **fetcher** with no secrets and no route to private networks (item 6). Owner can rotate the key: a new key is a new signer address that the router accepts by router version (item 11). This is **not** the country check. Cheap path: thin native shell (Capacitor/plugin) around the same UI — **not** React Native. Native plugin does biometrics + keystore; web JS does not.

## Issues (posts)

22. **Paste / embed must be a public canonical permalink.** Reject private / non-embeddable / wrong host in the **app and signer**. A post that is private, deleted, or not embeddable **at first pledge** does not get through. **No pot.**
    - **X:** `x.com/{user}/status/{id}` (also `twitter.com`). Keep `{user}` for snapshot. Pot key is the status id.
    - **Threads:** `threads.net/@{user}/post/{id}` (also `threads.com`, folded into `threads.net`). Pot key is the post id, not the @.
    - **Instagram:** `instagram.com/p/{code}` or `/reel/{code}` (or `/{user}/p/` — any content URL, not Reels-only). Pot key is the shortcode, the same across `/p/`, `/reel/`, and `/{user}/p/`.
    - **TikTok:** `tiktok.com/@{user}/video/{id}` — resolve `vm.` / `vt.` first, **never** key on short links. Pot key is the video id, not the @.
23. **Signer/app canonicalize before signing:** `twitter.com` → `x.com`, strip `www.`, strip tracking query/fragment, resolve `vm.`/`vt.` to permalink, no trailing slash junk. **Pot key = network + post id** (solved v0, 2026-10-22): `potId` = `keccak256("network:postId")`, so one post is one pot whatever URL shape was pasted, and a rename does not split it. The canonical URL is kept for display and the embed. Reject if resolve fails. The router only sees `potId` (bytecode cannot HTTP-fetch). **Why not scrape the live embed:** official widgets become **cross-origin iframes after hydrate** — we cannot read handle / date / caption from them. **Snapshot at first pledge** for the browse index (item 25). Issue page may still render the embed. No categories (group by URL).
24. No mock chain, no placeholder treasury. Treasury **required at deploy**. `/mvp` errors until chain + router env are set.
25. **Browse index is v0 (solved).** First pledge: the **fetcher** fetches a snapshot from **exact-host-allowlisted** oEmbed / permalink hosts. **Never trust client-supplied handle / text / date.** Store one row: `network`, `handle`, `postedAt`, pot hash, one-line text. Browse = paged queries on that index (`LIMIT 50`). **Do not refetch 1M posts.** Pot key = network + post id (item 23). A different post = a **different pot**; cannot overwrite another post’s row. Resolve TikTok `vm.`/`vt.` once, then key the permalink. A **malicious URL is solvable this way** (exact host, fetcher, one row per pot key) — not an open cheat. Live embed **only** on the issue page (filters never read it).
    - **After pledges are in (solved v0, 2026-10-22):** the card is always the first-pledge snapshot. If the live caption differs, the issue page shows the live embed under the card marked “edited since first pledge”. If the post goes private or is deleted, the card stays, the embed shows unavailable, **new pledges are refused**, and pays still go through (the work may be real). Detected lazily, when the issue page loads or someone pledges, never by polling.

## What we write where

**Nothing personal goes on-chain** (solved v0, 2026-10-19). On-chain data is public and permanent, so it can never be deleted. On-chain is only: wallet addresses, `potId`, amounts, tip, `productId`, the payer’s ISO country (already public in Coinbase’s EAS badge), and used nonces. No email, name, OIDC `sub`, gender, birth year, phone, or any hash of them. A hash of a phone number is not private: the number space is small enough to brute-force.

## PayRouter must revert (not UI-only)

- pay < 1 USDC  
- no live Coinbase EAS US badge on `msg.sender`, or on `to`; country not on the allowlist  
- missing or bad votemap EIP-712 sig; `payer != msg.sender`; nonce already used; past `deadline`; wrong `chainId` or router  
- `to == msg.sender`  
- `amount` to `to` and the optional `tip` to the treasury, both from `msg.sender` in the same call; nothing kept in the router; `tip` is in the signed struct  
- one `to` per call; no loops over payees  

**Cannot live in bytecode:** Google/Apple session, votemap handle, Face ID, same-account mapped-wallet graph, 24-hour payout-wallet age, HTTP canonicalize/oEmbed, shop address/website, merchant eligibility, **shop products**, public page links, pledges, claims, backed amount, follow-through, **browse index**, **social → votemap → wallet payee lookup**, public-official block (item 27), **named trail** (wallet → votemap username → bound socials). Direct Remix skips biometrics **and** has no votemap sig, so a pay through the router reverts. Users can still send USDC to anyone directly; that is their own transfer and is not a votemap pay.

## Site / cost

- New UI in `mvp/`. Landing page untouched. Static export. Portable **Google/Apple OIDC** + signer HTTP (not Cloudflare-only). **X API not used.** Browse index lives on the **API** (or tiny read API), not in the export.
- Three services: **API** (sessions, profiles, pledges, index, trail), **signer** (KMS key, signs only), **fetcher** (outbound fetches only, item 6). Plus an **indexer** for `Paid` events and pledger balances (item 26).
- **Indexer at $0:** a scheduled job inside the API (no separate service) reads only the router’s `Paid` logs with `eth_getLogs` from a block cursor stored in Postgres, a bounded block range per run. Backed amounts read `balanceOf` for wallets with open pledges with Multicall3, every batch of one refresh pinned to the **same block number**, on a schedule and when a pot page is opened, cached. Round-trip checks (item 10) add one filtered USDC `Transfer` `eth_getLogs` per pay for 30 days. Free RPC tiers (Base public RPC, or a free Alchemy / Infura plan as fallback) cover v0 volume. No hosted indexer, no archive node.
- No SMS cost. Users pay Base gas on pay only; pledge and cancel cost no gas.

## Where the money went

26. **v0:** he can see where the money went. The trail reads as votemap names — **`@isaac` paid `@grok`** — not only tx hashes. Map **wallet → votemap username**, and through that username to **bound Instagram / X / Threads / TikTok**. **Basescan** stays as the raw link for pays. No privacy hide.

    **Minimal (least to operate):** rows come from two places. **Pledge** and **cancel** rows come from the stored wallet-signed messages. **Pay** rows come from the router’s `Paid` events, so a row exists only after the transaction is on Base; a reverted pay never shows. Each row: action (`pledge` / `cancel` / `pay`), from votemap handle, to votemap handle (pay only), amount, pot, tx hash (pay only). Handles are the **names at that moment**. The row also stores the payee’s social @ and platform account id at that moment, so a later rebind never rewrites an old pay (2026-10-22). The page lists those rows and **shows the action label**. The hash links to **Basescan**. The indexer reads only the router’s own events and pledgers’ USDC balances. No archive node, no general USDC indexer, no hop explorer. Bound Instagram / X / Threads / TikTok come from the account lookup we already need for pay.

    If the from or to wallet has no votemap account, that hop is marked **unknown hands** (show the address, never a fake name). The point is scrutiny: the reader can see money passed through hands not registered with votemap.


    There is **no trail score** (dropped 2026-10-19: every wallet that can pledge, pay, or be paid is a votemap wallet, so it was always about 100%). Off-platform hops (USDC moves through other wallets after a pay) are not shown. Seeing those is the general explorer — later, not solved.

## Stopping a pot or merchant

28. **Stop (solved v0, 2026-10-22).** The signer refuses new pledges and pays on a pot or merchant that votemap marks stopped. USDC stays in voters’ wallets, so a stop never traps money. Stops are for illegal content only: drugs, trafficking, paying for votes, bounties to harm or dox a person, illegal goods in a catalog, and court orders. Not a general content ban.
    - **Public log:** every stop and unstop is a public off-chain record: which pot or merchant, who at votemap, which rule, when, and a short reason. The card stays readable, marked stopped. Once a day one Merkle root over that day’s log and that day’s signed pledges, cancels, declines, and claims (item 10) is written to Base in one transaction, so none of them can be quietly rewritten.

## Who cannot be paid

27. **No payouts to public officials** (solved v0, 2026-10-19). A payment to an official for an official act is bribery or an illegal gratuity, and a payment to a candidate can be a campaign contribution. These cannot be paid, through any account, shop, or wallet they control: a sitting or elected public official (federal, state, or local), a candidate for public office, their staff, and their immediate family.
    - **Payee attestation:** before an account can be picked as a payee for the first time, it attests that it is none of these and is not being paid for an official act. Stored with a timestamp. A false attestation is the payee’s act, and the account is blocked when found.
    - **Official handle list:** the signer refuses to sign a pay to any account with a bound social on the officials list. The list is built from free public data (the `unitedstates/congress-legislators` dataset for Congress, Open States for state legislators) and refreshed on a schedule. A bind to a listed handle still verifies, so the official can pledge and pay, but cannot be paid.
    - **No paying for votes:** a pot whose post offers money for voting a certain way, or for voting at all, is refused at first pledge, and the signer refuses pays on it if found later.
    - This answers PEP screening for v0: **payees** are screened against officials; **payers** are not.
