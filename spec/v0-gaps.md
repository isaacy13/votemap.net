# What v0 does not solve

Moved. Open questions: [open.md](./open.md). Solved (v0 only): [requirements.md](./requirements.md). Later (not solved, unfinished): [later.md](./later.md). Do not list open questions here.

Website verification, the 60-day lag, and shop bounty eligibility are solved v0 in requirements, not gaps. A shop cannot receive a bounty unless it has at least one verified link and zero unverified links. An address-only shop or any unverified link cannot. Legal name is not a signup field. VoteMap does not run its own sanctions list. Whether to screen politically exposed persons is open ([open.md](./open.md)). A politician claiming a bounty stays open.
