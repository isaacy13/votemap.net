# What Isaac is talking about on X

A capture of the votemap idea pile — not a plan, not a roadmap, not a commitment list.

**Locked production v0** is [requirements.md](./requirements.md), not this pile. v0 has **no custody** (2026-10-19): pledges are wallet-signed promises, pays go straight from the voter’s wallet to the payee through a PayRouter that holds nothing, **1.5% on pay, 0% on pledge / cancel / lapse**, no escrow, no shop credit, no vest. Pots show backed USDC and each account shows follow-through. Public officials cannot be paid. Nothing personal goes on-chain. **Bio** bind **verified for 60 days**; **pay picker** = verified social @ → payout wallet; **public page** `votemap.net/@username` (links **and shop catalog**). Named trail is v0 (pledge / cancel / pay rows, **unknown hands** if no account, hash → Basescan). Vendor KYC / license and a **general hop explorer** are **later**. Thread-era ideas below (escrow, time-bomb cuts, “10% agree → $1”) are not v0. [cheats-and-fixes.md](./cheats-and-fixes.md).

## What votemap is aiming at (in Isaac’s terms)

**Vote + roadmap.** Money as a tool to decide the future; people vote toward “compelling futures,” not just candidates.

The slogan he keeps returning to is **democratize everything**. Tech already spread fame (social media) and riches (a worldwide audience). Power is the leftover desire, “reserved for the rich.” Cancel culture is his sarcastic maybe. votemap is supposed to be the actual answer.

He also names three “core principles,” half-joking that the first might be a made-up word:

1. **Ideaocracy** — great ideas should shape the future.
2. **Meritocracy** — people who implement those ideas should be rewarded.
3. **True democracy** — everyone has a say, vs today’s representative setup.

The mechanism he keeps circling is blunt: pooled public money, held until a problem is actually solved, then paid to whoever solved it. He calls that “legalize bribery” — beat mega-donors at a game they already play behind closed doors. Crypto (dollar-backed stablecoins, not “a scam coin”) is there so voters can **see where the money went**. *(Thread-era. v0: signer one-row trail with **action** label stake / disperse / move / redeem; **unknown hands** if no account; visible-only score on those rows; hash → Basescan. A general hop explorer is **later**.)*

votemap is a **platform between ideas and problem solvers**. It is **not**, in his words, a replacement for government elections — “just voting after ppl get elected to make sure they do their jobs.”

## Who it’s for

- **Everyday people** — “paycheck to paycheck” voters who currently donate to campaigns, call a representative’s office, or have no systematic way to complain. He wants $1–$10/month to feel normal (Netflix/Starbucks money). He says he will put **$10k** of his own on issues he wants solved.
- **Problem-solvers** — students, working-class people, builders, startups, even a MrBeast-style bounty to stop child labor in chocolate. Celebrate them; “root out the grifters.”
- **Entities that should have to earn the money** — politicians, companies, churches, nonprofits, government agencies, indie creators, sports teams, artists, small shops. First shop he names as a pilot: **@onexengineering** (what to import from Japan; vote money as a down payment, held so the shop can’t take it and ghost).
- **Distribution crowd** — X and/or Threads (X first). He tags George Hotz, Elon, X Developers, Nikita Bier: “pretty much only X is in a position to do this,” while also saying @vote_map would start from scratch.
- **Later: everyone** — “regardless of political system,” starting with partners he names as Canada, Europe, Japan. Eventually “8.3 billion.” Also “the world’s most powerful companies,” who need to see that this works.

He is not trying to convert anyone’s party. Repeatedly: zero interest in swaying political beliefs; in the business of solving problems.

## The core problem / change

**Problem he keeps naming:** people with money (therefore power) decide history. Representative democracy + a two-party squeeze leaves almost no nuance and almost no feedback between elections. Politicians take donations on sweet promises, sit on boards, steer contracts to mega-donors, trade on insider info — “legalized stealing.” Lobbying is the existing bribe, quoted around **$4.5B/year**. Ordinary people have no matching pool.

**Change he wants:** collect small amounts from a huge number of people, convert that into **bounties**, and **only pay when the thing is done**. On-screen math in the video: ~342M people × ~$1.10/month ≈ $4.5B/year, dangled monthly (~$376M) over politicians’ heads until they cave. Stop donating directly to the “poor multi-millionaire politicians.” Same pattern for companies, charities, cities, schools, neighborhoods — not only federal politics.

He treats this as an **engineering and distribution problem**, not a left/right argument. “Screw political ideology.”

## Idea clusters

Loose piles from the thread. Many contradict each other; that is the point of this list.

### 1. Outcome-conditioned money (“legalize bribery”)

The spine. Voters put money on issues; solvers get it when voters agree the work is done. Politicians get paid when a bill actually passes / campaign promises are kept. Donations stay closed unless the entity answers “what did you get done this month?” Tiered payouts are floated (10% agree → $1, 20% → $10, “decided by voter”). Escrow comes up so entities cannot grab capital and vanish.

### 2. Transparency, crypto, “under the table”

Original goal: make rich-to-politician money traceable. Churches → nonprofits, agency spend, “20% of profits to charity” claims — all with an audit trail. Stripe-competitor flavor: same checkout, actual proof the donation happened. Medical receipts with proof a $100k scan really cost $100k. Everyone gets a **transparency score** for how on-chain they are, “especially politicians.”

Crypto is “meaningfully useful here”; he also says **gotta iron details out**.

### 3. Community feedback, then capital

First stated product: entities (companies, agencies, indie creators) get a place for community votes — optional paid votes to cut spam and “put money where mouth is.” Donation money as **working capital** for small experiments (cactus-flavored donuts). The entity can treat the vote as a gift, or let the voter redeem it as store credit when the thing exists.

### 4. Surfaces that are not “politics app”

He keeps attaching votemap to other products, as analogies more than specs:

- Linktree/Instagram: entity hub + vote on pinned topics. *(Thread-era. v0 public page is `votemap.net/@username`, links **and shop catalog** — [requirements](./requirements.md) item 6.)*
- Shopify/Squarespace: **voter-driven stores** (forecast demand instead of overproducing fast fashion). *(Thread-era. v0 shop is a catalog on `votemap.net/@username`, not a Shopify clone.)*
- Sports: fans vote trades.
- Stock exchange someday: “if Apple ships X, I’ll buy $Y of shares.”
- Government tax integration someday: e.g. 33% of taxes as votemap credits — “no taxation w/o representation.”
- Promo examples: ban politicians’ family stock trading; Texas digital ID and/or high-speed rail; fix potholes; “free newjeans or something.”

### 5. Identity, spam, and who sits at the table

Lo-fi MVP he scribbles: GitHub issues/board + ID + Stripe. Require Face ID / driver’s license. Show age, state, last verified date, X/IG/Threads handle. **Name optional.** *(Thread-era. v0 is Google/Apple + SMS + Coinbase EAS US + bio bind; vendor license is **later**.)* Monetize votes to reduce spam. “Free and low-price voting so that everyone gets a seat” lives in the README more than the thread; the thread is heavier on paid, identified votes.

### 6. Mechanics for not getting scammed (very unfinished)

- Market-style prize pools; winning side splits; maybe funded by the losing side; maybe a charity slice of the losing side.
- Time bombs: vote for a date; if it fails, money may be **unretrievable**; votemap might take ~10%, rest to a charity the user picks. *(Thread-era; locked v0 is 5% expiry with the rest back to the staker — [requirements](./requirements.md).)*
- Withdrawal tax / 3% if you pull votemap money off-chain (on the *next* transaction); “think up other ways.”
- Don’t let individuals resolve YES/NO in a way that always hits NO — **shaming** (A-tier voter vs F-tier scam voter) so entities can see if a bounty is real.
- Group unlock: cannot pull out unless >50% of users **or** 50% of the money votes to pull; then a 7-day window. So solvers have some confidence the pot won’t vanish.
- Anti-sandbagging: maximize competition so someone else takes the money if you stall.

### 7. Discourse, AI, and “use your brain”

Democracy “doesn’t work unless everyone uses their brain.” Collectivism only matters if people think for themselves; two or three opinions per issue is too few. AI page: “what did you get done?” — summaries of what politicians actually achieved, plus Q&A, because nobody will read the papers. Ad tones he likes: “your vote doesn’t matter,” “you’re all wrong,” “I’m betting $10k that everybody’s wrong, and I’m right.” “votemap is yc for people who solve real problems /jk.”

### 8. Longer horizon he does not pretend to have designed

Whoever controls robots, AI, and energy will rule; those need to be democratized/socialized “in some respect.” **Money will be irrelevant**, so votemap “will eventually need to evolve.” Yield on idle pots (treasuries, staking, etc.) is tempting, but a gambling/market overlay might make people chase money instead of outcomes. Prediction markets (Polymarket/Kalshi) tell you what *will* happen; votemap is supposed to tell you what *should*.

## Tensions / unresolved choices

These sit in the thread as-is. He does not pick.

- **Bribery as the honest name** vs wanting public trust and “true democracy.” He knows it sounds stupid; he leans into it anyway because lobbying already is bribery.
- **Pay-to-vote / bigger seat if you pay more** vs “everyone has a say.” He wants both: tiny monthly amounts so the crowd outguns elites, and identified paid votes so it isn’t spam.
- **Not replacing elections** vs “true democracy” and even a tax-credit voting layer.
- **X should build this** vs votemap starts from scratch vs “please steal this idea.”
- **Money as capital for the entity** vs **escrow held by votemap** so they cannot scam. Donut-credit vs Japan-import down payment are different trust models.
- **Easy pull-out** vs **locked pots / time bombs / majority-to-withdraw.** One cluster protects voters; the other protects solvers.
- **Market/prize/yield incentives** vs “if you did some gambling/market system, then people are focused on making money, not making a difference.”
- **Deep individual thinking** vs **large-sample collectivism.** He wants both and says the current 2–3-opinion world fails both.
- **Open source forever / light it on fire if closed** vs a platform that also needs to be “highly available, reliable, and trustworthy” and to win over the most powerful companies.
- **Compete with Linktree, Shopify, Stripe, GitHub issues** vs “the mission behind the products are way different.”
- **Celebrate problem-solvers, not politicians** vs paying politicians (and even a voter-chosen split of the pot between “the government” and “the politicians that got it done”).
- **On-chain purity / transparency scores** vs the fact that most people will not live fully on-chain; hence fees and shame as patches.

## Explicitly not decided yet

He says this out loud, a lot.

- Almost every **mechanism knob**: payout thresholds, who funds prize pools, charity percentages, withdrawal tax “Z%,” 50% of users vs 50% of dollars, time-bomb rules, how to keep money on-chain besides a 3% slap.
- **Crypto details** — “gotta iron details out.” Dollar-backed stablecoin is the intent, not a design.
- **Identity vs anonymity** beyond “name optional, license required.” *(Thread. v0 is Coinbase EAS US + bio; vendor license is later.)*
- **What votemap becomes when money stops mattering.**
- **Whether this is a product, a movement, or bait for X/someone else to build.** The explainer is him announcing with, in his words, **zero lines of code and no Venn diagram**, hoping someone competent steals it; otherwise he will build it anyway.
- **Builder compensation.** He asks for unpaid fintech/crypto people for now; “hopefully” paid later; no promise.
- **When he personally builds.** In the Feb 2026 video he says he does not expect meaningful votemap time until after June 2026 (SkyI). That is a personal-calendar remark in an outreach video, not a product schedule — and he still kept adding to the idea thread after that.
- **How each non-politics surface actually works** (sports votes, stock-conditional buys, tax credits, medical proof). Named, not specified.
- **The “next video”** that was supposed to walk issue-by-issue solutions. Not in the thread, not in this explainer.

If a later bookmarks dump or that next video shows up, it should be read against this pile — not treated as if the pile were already a spec.
